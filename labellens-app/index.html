How it works

1. Snap

Take a photo of the label, or upload one.

2. Read

An open AI model (Gemma 4) reads the text, in the language printed.

3. Check

Our code checks the answer. Blurry or unsure photos never get an OK. You get a retake request.

Honest limits

  • It can be wrong. Always check the real label.
  • It never gives a medicine or pesticide dose.
  • It only compares claims with the text printed on the pack.
  • Photos are sent to an AI service to be read.
